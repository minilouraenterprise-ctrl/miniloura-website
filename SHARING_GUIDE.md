# 🌐 Share Your Miniloura Website

Your website is currently local (only on your computer). To share it with others, you need to publish it online. Here are the easiest ways:

## 🚀 **Easiest: Drag & Drop Hosting**

### **Netlify (Recommended)**
1. Go to [netlify.com](https://netlify.com)
2. Sign up for free
3. Drag your entire `windsurf-project` folder onto the page
4. **Done!** You'll get a shareable link like `https://your-name.netlify.app`

### **Vercel**
1. Go to [vercel.com](https://vercel.com)
2. Sign up for free
3. Click "New Project" → "Import Git Repository" → "Browse"
4. Select your `windsurf-project` folder
5. Deploy → Get your link instantly

## 💰 **100% Free Options**

### **GitHub Pages**
1. Create account at [github.com](https://github.com)
2. Create new repository named `miniloura-website`
3. Upload all your files
4. Go to Settings → Pages → Source: Deploy from branch
5. Your site: `https://yourname.github.io/miniloura-website`

### **Firebase Hosting**
1. Go to [firebase.google.com](https://firebase.google.com)
2. Create free account
3. Start new project → Hosting
4. Upload your files
5. Get your free domain

## 🎯 **Quick Share Method**

### **CodePen (for demo)**
1. Go to [codepen.io](https://codepen.io)
2. Create new Pen
3. Copy-paste your HTML, CSS, JS
4. Share the CodePen link
- *Good for showing, not for permanent business site*
